with none. A gate that prints VALIDATED means nothing until you have watched
it print NOT VALIDATED on data where nothing is there.

The win run uses --seed 1. Seed 7 at the same true preference produced 22 of 35
and FAILED clause 2 - a genuine 2% tail draw, not a bug, and a useful reminder
that this criterion can miss a real effect. See tools/power.py: at 35
completions clause 2 detects a true 80% preference 97% of the time but a true
70% preference only 65% of the time.

  is detected 97% of the time, but a moderately better one (70%) is missed

  pooled preference in the 60s, the honest reading is 'underpowered', not
  'no better' - and the writeup should say so.
