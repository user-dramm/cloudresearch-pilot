Before you start

Watch two short training videos and tell us what you think

You'll watch two short training videos, about 9 minutes each, and answer a few quick questions about each one. At the end you'll say which of the two you thought was better and why.

What to expect

  • Plan for roughly 25 minutes altogether, in one sitting.
  • Each video runs in two parts with a short black pause between them. Keep watching when the screen goes dark. It says "Part 2" and carries on.
  • The questions unlock once a video has actually played through, so plan for the full time. Jumping ahead won't work: the player rewinds you to where you got to.
  • Partway through the second part of each video a short code word appears on screen. There's a box right under the video, so type it in as soon as you see it and you don't have to remember it.
  • After each video there are four quick questions. The writing, if you want to do any, is all at the end.
  • Your progress is saved as you go. If your browser closes or you lose power, reopen this same link and you'll carry on from where you stopped, without having to rewatch.
  • Your written comments matter most. The ratings tell us that something worked; only your own words tell us why, and that is the part we cannot get any other way.
Answer honestly. There are no right or wrong opinions, and any view is accepted. Rushed or copy-pasted answers may be rejected. At the end you'll get a completion code to paste back into CloudResearch Connect.
